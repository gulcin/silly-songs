# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-19

- Ten blank songbook pages that stay saved on this device, even after the tab is closed.
- A song title and lyrics on every page, with round, hand, and story lettering and five ink colours.
- Videos from the photo library or the camera, playing on the page they were added to.
- Save to Photos: a picture of the words, and each video, with a download if sharing is not available.
- Send to Mum on every screen.
- A home-screen app that can open again offline, and that updates in place without getting stuck.
